Spinoza

Reading tools for the Ethics and a kit for building a class reading portal.

The Portal Search and read a text corpus, follow connections between texts, and collect passages into handouts. Sample corpus: Ethics I. Scribano: Guida alla lettura dell’Etica di Spinoza A single-page reader with contents, search, dark mode and saved reading position. PDF Reader Open a PDF from your computer: contents, thumbnails, zoom, notes by page and search. Nothing is uploaded. Method Log One entry per session: what was tried, and whether it worked.
The Lab Kit, for starting a portal of your own, is in lab-kit/ on GitHub.
